Kit that fits the training

FighterBase is the training and club app for UK combat-sports gyms. We show members the right kit at the moment they need it, from shops we trust.

What FighterBase is

Clubs teaching Muay Thai, boxing, kickboxing, BJJ, no-gi and MMA run their timetable, bookings, attendance and member payments in FighterBase.

Their adult members use it to book classes, log training and sparring, follow fight camps, learn technique and track their progress.

Because the app knows what someone trains and what they've booked, a kit suggestion can be useful rather than random. Someone booked into their first sparring class sees a gum shield and shin guards, not another pair of shorts.

How partner products appear

  • In the Kit shop and beside training. Members see kit for the class they've booked, starter kits for their sport, and one rotating kit pick on their training pages. They can hide the picks.
  • Clearly labelled. Every product card says Ad and that we may earn commission. The partner shop sells, ships and handles returns.
  • Each club has its own shop. A club can switch it on or off and add its coach's picks with a reason for each.
  • Adults only. Nothing is shown to under-18s or in a club's kids academy.
  • Never next to health and safety. No products beside food diaries, weight cuts, injuries, concussion or safety screens.
  • Nutrition comes later, and carefully. Only batch-tested products (for example Informed Sport), only after our dietitian has reviewed them, and only the health claims the product is allowed to make.

What we never share

  • No personal data in links. A link carries only an anonymous club code, never a member's name, email or account.
  • Clubs see totals, not people. A club can see how many of its members looked at its shop, never who looked or who bought.
  • Your products, shown as they are. We show the name, size, price, shop and the date the price was checked, from your own product feed.
