What FighterBase is

FighterBase is training and club software for UK combat-sports gyms: Muay Thai, boxing, kickboxing, BJJ, no-gi and MMA. Clubs run their classes and members in it, and their adult members use it to book classes and log their training.

How partner products appear

  • Clearly labelled. Every partner product says Ad and that we may earn a commission. The partner shop sells, delivers and handles returns.
  • Adults only. Nothing is shown to under-18s.
  • No personal data in links. A link never carries a member's name, email or account.
  • Your products, shown as they are. Name, size, price and shop, from your own product feed.
